Astronomical Discovery: James Webb Space Telescope Detects Atmospheric Water Vapor on Exoplanet. Spectroscopic observations from the James Webb Space Telescope have revealed unambiguous signatures of atmospheric water vapor and carbon dioxide on a sub-Neptune exoplanet orbiting within the habitable zone of a nearby red dwarf star. Transmission spectroscopy collected during planetary transit confirms the presence of reflective clouds at high altitudes, providing crucial insights into planetary formation and chemical evolution in distant solar systems.
